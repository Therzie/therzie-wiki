# Therzie Mods Wiki

The wiki for the Therzie mods for Valheim: weapons, armor, creatures, food and progression,
with search, filters and a progression view per biome.

The content is based on the Therzie Mods Wiki spreadsheet by **Majestic**. Numbers and recipes are
imported from the spreadsheet; descriptions, notes and tips are written by the editors.

## Contents

| Mod | What you find |
|---|---|
| Warfare | Weapons, shields, ammo, pieces and items |
| Armory | Armor sets with set effects and upgrades |
| Monstrum | Creatures, bosses, weaknesses and food |
| Wizardry | Stations, staffs, robes, rings, circlets, creatures, food and potions |
