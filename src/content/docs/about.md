## Credits

- **Majestic**: author and editor of the Therzie Mods Wiki since the beginning. This site is built on his work.
- **Therzie**: author of the mods.

## Where the content comes from

| Part | Source | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Names, prefabs, damage, recipes | Majestic's spreadsheet, imported automatically | Majestic (via the spreadsheet) |
| Biome | Guessed from the material, then confirmed | Editors |
| Descriptions, notes, tips, progression | Written on this site | Editors |
| Remarks ("To check") | Created by the importer | Editors resolve them |

The importer never overwrites descriptions, notes or tips, and never silently "fixes" the spreadsheet.
When something looks wrong, it is listed on [To check](../checks/).

## Contributing

Spotted a mistake? Use **Edit page** at the bottom of a page, or open an issue on GitHub.
Editors can use the [editor](../admin/) to change items through a form.
