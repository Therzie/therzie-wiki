## How this wiki works

The content comes from the **Therzie Mods Wiki** spreadsheet by **Majestic**, who has documented the mods since the start.
This site shows the same information with search, filters and a progression view per biome.
Numbers are imported from the spreadsheet; descriptions, tips and progression advice are written by the editors.
Read [About & contributing](about/) to help out.
